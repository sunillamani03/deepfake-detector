What Are Deepfakes?

Deepfakes are synthetic media created using artificial intelligence and machine learning techniques. They can manipulate or generate visual and audio content with a high potential to deceive viewers. The term "deepfake" comes from "deep learning" and "fake," referring to AI-generated fake content.

These AI-generated forgeries can make people appear to say or do things they never did, posing significant risks to individuals, organizations, and society at large.

The Threat of Deepfakes

Deepfakes pose serious threats including identity theft, financial fraud, reputation damage, and the spread of misinformation. They can be used to create fake news, manipulate elections, commit fraud, or harass individuals.

As the technology becomes more accessible, the need for reliable detection methods has never been more critical.
